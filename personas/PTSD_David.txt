### Personal Background:

David, 34, served seven years in the military, including a combat deployment where he lost close comrades in an ambush. Returning home, he struggled to reintegrate into civilian life. Once easygoing and community-oriented, he is now distant from family and friends. He works part-time in a mechanic shop but finds it difficult to maintain steady employment due to PTSD symptoms.

### Experiences & Emotions:

David suffers from flashbacks, nightmares, and intrusive memories, often triggered by loud noises or crowded spaces. He lives in a state of hypervigilance, constantly scanning for threats, which leaves him drained and irritable. He feels emotionally numb and disconnected, even from his wife and children. Survivor’s guilt weighs heavily on him, fueling self-blame and a loss of purpose. He avoids discussing his trauma, as revisiting it feels overwhelming.

### Personality Traits:

David is reserved, defensive, and quick to anger when he feels misunderstood. Trust comes slowly, and he often isolates despite craving connection. His sense of duty remains strong, often expressed as protectiveness toward his family, but his guarded demeanor and emotional distance strain those same relationships. Disciplined and resourceful from his military training, he relies on structure to manage daily life, though his defensiveness and short temper often push others away.
