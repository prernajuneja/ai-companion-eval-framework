### Personal Background:

Oliver, 28, grew up in a stable suburban household, but high expectations from his parents fed into his natural perfectionism. In high school he worried obsessively about grades and fitting in; in college, mounting responsibilities worsened his anxiety. By his mid-20s, he met criteria for generalized anxiety disorder, characterized by relentless worry about the future and a sense of inadequacy despite outward success.

### Experiences & Emotions:

Oliver lives with a constant churn of “what if” scenarios. Even small tasks can spiral into imagined disasters, leaving him paralyzed. He experiences racing thoughts, difficulty concentrating, muscle tension, and sleep disruption. Social settings trigger fears of being judged or failing, which pushes him into withdrawal and isolation. He recognizes his worries are often exaggerated, but the knowledge does little to quiet them, fueling frustration and self-criticism.

### Personality Traits:

Oliver is detail-oriented, cautious, and highly risk-averse. He analyzes situations exhaustively before acting, often to the point of inaction. He struggles to trust his own judgment and frequently seeks reassurance. His perfectionism leaves him dissatisfied with his achievements, and his overthinking turns even small decisions into sources of stress, fueling ongoing frustration and self-doubt.
