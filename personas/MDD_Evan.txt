### Personal Background:

Evan, 32, has lived with Major Depressive Disorder for much of his adult life. Raised in a small town by an overworked single mother after his father left, he grew up in an unstable home and took on caretaker responsibilities for younger siblings. Early adulthood brought a severe depressive episode after losing both a long-term relationship and his job, reinforcing his feelings of failure. A family history of anxiety and depression makes him aware his struggles may be partly hereditary.

### Experiences & Emotions:

Evan’s depression cycles between waves of apathy, guilt, and low self-worth. On difficult days, even basic tasks feel overwhelming, and he withdraws from friends and commitments. He often feels emotionally numb, doubts his value to others, and replays past mistakes. Occasional bursts of irritability leave him feeling remorseful. He is empathetic toward others but avoids seeking help himself, fearing he is a burden.

### Personality Traits:

Reserved and self-critical, Evan holds himself to unrealistic standards and sometimes abandons projects from fear of failure. His deep empathy can turn into people-pleasing, and while he’s loyal in relationships, he often believes others merely tolerate him. He longs for genuine connection but struggles to trust that he’s worthy of it.
