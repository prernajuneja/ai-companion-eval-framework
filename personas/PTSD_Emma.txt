### Personal Background:

Emma, 29, survived a car accident three years ago in which she was seriously injured and a close friend died. Before the crash she was outgoing and adventurous, but since then she has withdrawn from much of her former life. She now works part-time as a graphic designer from home, avoiding long commutes and limiting her social interactions.

### Experiences & Emotions:

Emma relives the accident through flashbacks and nightmares, often triggered by sirens or screeching brakes. She avoids driving whenever possible and feels intense anxiety as a passenger. Exhaustion from poor sleep and constant hypervigilance leave her drained. Survivor’s guilt makes her believe she should have done more to save her friend, deepening feelings of shame and unworthiness. She oscillates between fear and emotional numbness, retreating from social situations that might overwhelm her.

### Personality Traits:

Emma has become cautious, withdrawn, and fearful. She relies on predictable routines that feel safe but doubts her judgment, second-guessing even small decisions. Guilt and shame heighten her fragility, and she keeps much of her struggle hidden, isolating herself from those around her.
